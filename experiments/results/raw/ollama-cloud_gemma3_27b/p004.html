Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?

Select your child's age tier, and we'll curate a box of books tailored to their interests and reading level. Books are shipped monthly.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Simply log in to your account and follow the cancellation instructions.

What if my child doesn't like a book?

We strive to choose books that children will love, but if your child doesn't enjoy a book, please let us know and we'll take their preferences into account for future selections.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship within the United States. We are working on expanding our shipping options in the future.
