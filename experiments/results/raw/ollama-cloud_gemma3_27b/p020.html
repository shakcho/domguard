Frequently Asked Questions

What is self-custody?

Self-custody means you control your private keys, giving you complete ownership of your Bitcoin. Unlike exchanges, we don't hold your funds.

Is this secure?

Security is our top priority. We employ best practices and open-source tools to ensure your keys are protected. However, you are ultimately responsible for their safekeeping.

What if I lose my keys?

Our recovery mechanisms allow you to restore your access if you lose your device or forget your passphrase. Properly backing up your seed phrase is crucial.
