How does ObserverIQ handle data security?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. We comply with GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 standards. Our agents operate with minimal permissions and never collect sensitive business data without explicit configuration.

What integrations do you support?

We support 200+ integrations including AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, Docker, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Apache, Nginx, and all major APM tools. New integrations are added monthly based on customer demand.

We offer tiered pricing based on the number of hosts and data volume. Our plans start at $49/host/month for basic monitoring, with enterprise options for larger deployments. We also offer custom pricing for specific use cases.
