Frequently Asked Questions

How often will I receive books?

You'll receive a curated box of 2-3 books every month, tailored to your selected age group.

Can I change my subscription plan?

Yes! You can change your age tier or pause your subscription anytime through your account dashboard.

What if my child already has a book you send?

We maintain a diverse rotation and if you receive a duplicate, we'll happily replace it at no cost.

Is there a commitment period?

No! You can cancel your subscription anytime with no penalties or hidden fees.
