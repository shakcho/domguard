Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription work?

Choose your plan, select your meals each week, and we deliver everything you need right to your door. You can skip weeks or cancel anytime.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

We offer vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free options. You can filter our menu to find meals that meet your dietary needs.

How long do the ingredients stay fresh?

We deliver all ingredients in insulated boxes with ice packs. Most ingredients will stay fresh for 5-7 days when refrigerated properly.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, you can update your delivery address up to 5 days before your next scheduled delivery through your account settings.
